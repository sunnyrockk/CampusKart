About CampusKart

Better campus commerce starts here.

A thoughtful marketplace built to make student life more affordable, connected and sustainable.

Our mission

Make useful things go further.

CampusKart connects students with the books, devices and essentials they need—without the new-item price tag.

We believe every great textbook, calculator, and desk accessory deserves more than one semester. By keeping valuable items in circulation, our community saves money and cuts needless waste.

Mission

Make student essentials accessible to every learner.

Vision

A campus economy that is affordable and circular.
