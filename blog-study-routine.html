A kinder way to build a study routine

Consistency is not about having perfect days. It is about making the next session easy to start.

When a task feels large, motivation rarely arrives first. Action comes first. A modest and repeatable routine makes it easier to take that first step, especially during a busy semester.

Start smaller than you think

Choose one time, one location and one 25-minute task. Do not aim to master an entire unit; aim to solve two problems or revise one concept. Stopping with a clear next step makes returning tomorrow much easier.

Review instead of re-reading

Close the notes and retrieve what you remember. Make a quick question card. Explain a definition without looking. Retrieval feels harder than re-reading, but it gives you a much clearer picture of what has actually stayed with you.

Try this today

Set a 25-minute timer, put your phone out of reach, and write the one question you want to answer before you begin.
