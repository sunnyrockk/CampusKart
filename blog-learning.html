Why explaining an idea helps you understand it

Teaching is not reserved for experts. It is one of the best ways to become a better learner.

You may recognise a concept when you see it in your notes yet struggle to explain it without them. That gap is useful information. It shows where your understanding needs more structure, an example or a return to the source.

Make the explanation simple

Imagine speaking to a friend who has never seen the topic. Avoid specialist words at first. Use one everyday example. When an explanation relies on a phrase you cannot unpack, pause and investigate it.

Learning together is a superpower

Swap roles with a study partner: one person explains while the other asks questions. You do not need to be flawless. Honest questions make the explanation stronger and help both people remember the idea later.

Try this today

Record a one-minute voice note explaining a class concept, then listen back and note one part that could be clearer.
